LAGUNA VERDE

Carmen's Debt — an open world crime story

Carmen runs her late father's garage by the lagoon. Her little brother Tomas owes $50,000 to a crew called Los Tiburones after wrecking their car. She takes driving jobs across town to pay it off, one fare at a time, while the police watch every corner.

  • Drive and walk a full lagoon city: causeway, marina, boardwalk, Ferris wheel, lighthouse, downtown.
  • 7 story missions, traffic, pedestrians, a 5 star wanted level, police chases and a helicopter.
  • Steal cars, race, run getaways, buy garage upgrades, save your progress.
